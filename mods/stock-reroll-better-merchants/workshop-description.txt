[h1]Stock Reroll: Better Merchants Compatibility[/h1]

Press your Stock Reroll hotkey (F5 by default) to reroll stock in Better Merchants night shops. Each shop keeps its stock, pricing and nightly event rules. Your Stock Reroll fee and transaction protection settings apply on each visit.

Subscribe to and enable [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828934641]Stock Reroll 1.0.8[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834518042]Better Merchants 2.0.1[/url] and this patch.
