# 商品刷新：更好的商人兼容补丁 / Stock Reroll: Better Merchants Compatibility

同时启用商品刷新、Better Merchants 和本补丁。进入 Better Merchants 的夜间店铺后，按商品刷新的快捷键，默认是 F5。各店铺继续使用 Better Merchants 的供货、价格和当晚事件规则。成交保护与刷新费用沿用商品刷新的设置，从新一次到访重新计数。

Enable this patch alongside Stock Reroll and Better Merchants. In a Better Merchants night shop, press your Stock Reroll hotkey (F5 by default). Shops use Better Merchants' stock, prices, and nightly events. Transaction protection and reroll fees follow your Stock Reroll settings and reset on each visit.

实现核对基线为 Better Merchants 2.0.1。 The integration was checked against Better Merchants 2.0.1.
