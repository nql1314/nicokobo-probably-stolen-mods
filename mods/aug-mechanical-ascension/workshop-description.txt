[h1]伪人：机械飞升 / Aug: Mechanical Ascension[/h1]

[h2]简体中文[/h2]

[b]Mod 版本：1.0.0[/b]

以伪人身份经营当铺，管理身体状态、寻找材料修补身体，并逐步制造义体与脑机接口。

[list]
[*]独立机械飞升开局，包含健康、暴露与身体修补机制。
[*]七件义体、脑机接口、Nico 工坊和成就。
[*]灰线供货与“失物招领”调查剧情。
[*]认知涡轮可与模组矩阵的连续模组联动。
[/list]

[b]前置：NicoKobo Forge、Mechcore Synthesis 和 Mechcore Module Matrix。[/b]请分别安装并启用后重启游戏。适用于正式版内置 ModLoader。

[b]可选联动：食品加工。[/b]增加 K04 修补胶制造、压缩营养液修补及灰线食品加工器供货。未安装时两条 K04 配方不显示，相关灰线商品不供货；常规医疗材料及医疗掉落／供货的修补胶仍可使用。

[h2]English[/h2]

Version 1.0.0. Play an Aug starting scenario with body maintenance, cybernetics, a brain-computer interface, the Nico Workshop, achievements and the Lost and Found story. Requires NicoKobo Forge, Mechcore Synthesis and Mechcore Module Matrix. Enable all required mods and restart the game.

Food Processing is optional, adding K04 repair-gel crafting, Compressed Nutrient Liquid repairs and Food Processor stock from Grayline. Without it, the two K04 recipes are hidden and related Grayline stock is unavailable; native medical supplies and repair gel from medical loot or suppliers remain usable.
