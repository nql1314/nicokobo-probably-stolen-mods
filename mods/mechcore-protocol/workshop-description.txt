[h1]Mechcore Protocol: Aug Ascension[/h1]

Combines Mechcore Synthesis, Module Matrix and Aug: Mechanical Ascension in one mod. Build industrial equipment and cybernetics, develop machine modules and follow the Lost and Found story as an Aug shopkeeper.

Includes three industrial machines and Forge terminal recipes, manufactured materials and cybernetics, robots and utility devices; 18 active modules and nodes with six placement effects and continuous growth; the Aug starting scenario, survival, brain-computer interface, seven cybernetics, 13 workshop achievements, Grayline supply and four story endings.

The Material Refiner handles all four glass recycling recipes; Mechcore no longer extends the native furnace. S26 Batch Smelting is retired and no longer stocked, while existing items remain readable in saves. Multi-batch processing and Continuous Acceleration, Efficiency and Calibration modules remain available. All five nodes have standalone descriptions, without duplicate ability text.

Press G for recipe lookup. Open Nico Workshop by double-clicking its item, pressing N or dialing 1016.

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831025262]NicoKobo Forge[/url], installed and enabled separately.[/b] Food Processing and Automated Logistics are optional. Disable the three replaced component packages before enabling this mod, then restart.

This mod was developed following the official guide and has passed mod-scan checks. It requests no additional permissions, including external reflection access. Beyond the changes described on this page, it should generally leave base-game features unaffected. If a conflict occurs when using it alongside a third-party mod, please contact that mod's author first for help investigating.
